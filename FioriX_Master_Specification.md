# FioriX Master Specification

This is the master index for the pre-development project definition pack. The detailed specifications are split under `docs/`.

Read `README.md` first, then proceed through the numbered documents. The pack covers product scope, business and functional requirements, SAP and general technology stacks, architecture, integration, T-Code discovery, business analysis, Fiori catalogue and fit-gap, generation, AI/ML, database, API, UI/UX, security, validation, R&D, sprints, deployment and risks.

**Baseline:** S/4HANA On-Premise only; SAP BTP Cloud Foundry; SAPUI5 freestyle; CAP Node.js/TypeScript; HANA Cloud; approved ADT/OData/ABAP interfaces; AI optional; DMS conditional on entitlement; standard Fiori first; generated artifacts remain drafts pending SAP-side validation and human approval.

# Business Requirements
| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Reduce manual effort assessing SAP GUI transactions for Fiori adoption | Must | Complete pilot assessment |
| BR-02 | Evaluate standard Fiori before custom build | Must | Build requires catalogue and fit-gap evidence |
| BR-03 | Explain findings to business and technical users | Must | Summary plus drill-down |
| BR-04 | Capture company-specific rules | Must | Functional reviewer confirms |
| BR-05 | Identify partial and unsupported capabilities | Must | Evidence-backed fit-gap |
| BR-06 | Generate reviewable custom app draft where justified | Should | Validated reference pattern |
| BR-07 | Provide security, governance and audit | Must | Role and approval evidence |
| BR-08 | Expand to bulk and landscape scans | Later | Roadmap and job design |

Decision policy:
- ADOPT: standard app meets all critical confirmed requirements.
- EXTEND: standard app is suitable and supported extension mechanisms address gaps.
- BUILD: material requirements remain unmet and custom app is justified.
- REVIEW: requirements, evidence or compatibility are incomplete.

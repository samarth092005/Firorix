# Business and Functional Analysis
Capture process/sub-process, business purpose, activities, actors, preconditions, inputs, outputs, business objects, rules, validations, approvals, exceptions, recovery, custom enhancements and roles.

Requirement record: ID, statement, rationale, priority, source, acceptance criteria, related activity, confirmation status, reviewer and date.

Sources may include consultant entry, functional specification, interview notes or AI-proposed draft. Preserve source and confirmation state. AI cannot confirm business requirements.

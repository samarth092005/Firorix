# R&D Plan
## A. SAP technical discovery
Research T-Code metadata, ADT resources, repository object types, where-used, CDS, OData, authorization and source access. Prove one T-Code.

## B. Fiori catalogue
Research official Fiori Apps Reference Library, app IDs, versions, components, prerequisites, services and extensibility. Verify permitted catalogue acquisition/update method. Curate pilot sample.

## C. Business process
Interview functional reviewer; document purpose, actors, activities, inputs, outputs, rules, validations, approvals, exceptions and custom variants.

## D. Fit-gap
Create capability taxonomy, match statuses, evidence model and decision policy. Review sample cases.

## E. Generation
Verify target-release support for ABAP CDS, RAP, SRVD/SRVB, OData protocol, Fiori elements, annotations, activation and transport. Produce one reference app pattern.

## F. BTP architecture
Inspect CF quota, HANA Cloud, identity, destinations, connectivity, HTML5 repository, DMS, logging and audit entitlements.

## Deliverables
SAP capability report; ADT proof; verified Fiori sample; functional specification; fit-gap methodology; generator feasibility report; BTP inventory; security/storage decision; ADRs.

## Exit
No critical design is based only on assumption. Unsupported features are excluded or deferred.
